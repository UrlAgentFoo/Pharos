Pharos runs its tools on this computer through its local server. Start it with npx pharos-lab and open the address it prints.

Pharos finds the books that can run on your data.

Accepted data

  • Microscopy: TIFF, OME-TIFF, Zeiss .czi, Nikon .nd2, Leica .lif, OME-Zarr folders; 2D, 3D, channels and time
  • Simulation: OpenFOAM case folders (system/, constant/, 0/)
  • Tables (.csv, .parquet), meshes (.ply, .vtk) and videos (.mp4)

Folders (OpenFOAM cases, OME-Zarr) can't be dragged in yet: click the box above and choose the folder.
